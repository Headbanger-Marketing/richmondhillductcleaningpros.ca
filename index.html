FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

How often do Richmond Hill homes need duct cleaning?

Every two to three years for most, sooner with pets, allergy sufferers, or a renovation in the house's recent past. Given the turnover and finished basements common here, resale buyers should simply start with a full clean.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Yes, certified through training and fully insured on every job. Ontario issues no trade licence for duct cleaning, so certification and insurance are the credentials worth asking for, and we carry both without hesitation.

What proof do you provide that the work was done?

Photographs of your duct interiors, before and after, shown to you in your home before we pack up. We have never once asked a Richmond Hill client to take it on faith.

Our dryer smells hot and runs long. What does that mean?

Get it checked soon, because that combination says lint buildup restricting the vent. We brush the full run to the exterior hood and measure airflow, which tells you whether the machine or the pipe was the problem.

What happens during a whole-home cleaning and how long?

Vacuum connected at the furnace, registers removed and washed, every run brushed under suction, blower compartment cleaned, before and after photos throughout. Plan on three to four hours for a typical detached home here.

There is a musty smell when the furnace starts each fall.

That is residue and damp holding in the runs after a summer idle. A full cleaning removes it, and a sanitizer treatment applied to the clean ducts addresses anything remaining. The two steps together end the smell.

Why does dust return so fast in our house?

In this market it is usually return-side leaks drawing air from renovated basements or wall cavities. We pressure-check for leakage and seal it, because no cleaning schedule can outrun a duct system feeding itself dust.

Where do you work beyond Richmond Hill?

Across Richmond Hill including Oak Ridges and Thornhill, into Markham, Aurora, Newmarket, and King City. Call with your address if you are near the edges.

Do you serve areas outside of Richmond Hill, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Richmond Hill and nearby communities including Oak Ridges, Thornhill, Markham, Aurora, and the surrounding area.
